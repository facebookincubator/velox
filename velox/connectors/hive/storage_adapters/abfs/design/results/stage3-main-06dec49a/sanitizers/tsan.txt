Finding and repair
The first complete C3 TSAN run found a test-only race in
AbfsFileSystemTest.multipleThreadsWithReadFile. Ten workers captured one local
random engine and distribution by reference while choosing stagger delays.
Delay selection now occurs serially before each thread starts, and the worker
captures only its selected integer delay. A separate helper generator is also
thread-local. The focused multithreaded test, complete TSAN suites, 100-repeat
C3 matrix, final ASAN gates, and ordinary suites pass after the repair.
Production behavior did not change for this finding.
