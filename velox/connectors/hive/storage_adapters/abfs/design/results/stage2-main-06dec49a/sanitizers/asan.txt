Finding and repair
The first valid complete ASAN transport run found a heap-use-after-free in the
PoolCapSuspendsFiberUntilBodyRelease test harness. Its nested fiber captured an
outer-fiber-local shared transport and Azure request by reference. The outer
fiber could destroy those locals after releasing the first response body while
the nested fiber resumed and called Send. The test now captures the transport
by value and builds its own request from a copied URL. The focused test, both
complete suites, and both 100-repeat matrices pass after this repair.

No production transport behavior was changed for this finding.
